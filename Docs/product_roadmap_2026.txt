Product Roadmap 2026
=====================
Product: TechVenture Platform
Version: Roadmap v3.1
Owner: Product Strategy Team
Last Updated: October 2025

VISION
Become the leading AI-powered enterprise platform in the European market
by Q4 2026, serving 500 enterprise clients with 99.9 percent uptime.

Q1 2026 — FOUNDATION
Milestone 1.1: Migrate infrastructure to multi-region EU deployment (Jan 15)
Milestone 1.2: Launch SSO and enterprise authentication module (Jan 31)
Milestone 1.3: Complete SOC2 Type II certification (Feb 28)
Milestone 1.4: Release API v3.0 with GraphQL support (Mar 31)
Dependencies: DevOps team capacity, AWS EU region availability
Risk: Certification timeline may slip 3-4 weeks

Q2 2026 — GROWTH
Milestone 2.1: AI-assisted workflow automation module (Apr 30)
Milestone 2.2: Integration marketplace launch (15 connectors) (May 31)
Milestone 2.3: Mobile app release iOS and Android (Jun 15)
Milestone 2.4: German and French localization complete (Jun 30)
Dependencies: Mobile team hire Q1, localization vendor contract
Risk: Mobile launch complexity higher than estimated

Q3 2026 — SCALE
Milestone 3.1: Enterprise analytics dashboard v2 (Jul 31)
Milestone 3.2: Custom AI model fine-tuning feature (Aug 31)
Milestone 3.3: Partner API program launch (50 partners target) (Sep 30)
Dependencies: ML infrastructure upgrade, partner onboarding team

Q4 2026 — CONSOLIDATION
Milestone 4.1: Platform performance optimization sprint (Oct)
Milestone 4.2: Annual enterprise client review and renewal campaign (Nov)
Milestone 4.3: 2027 roadmap planning and board presentation (Dec)

KEY METRICS TO TRACK
- Monthly Active Users: target 12,000 by Q4 2026
- Enterprise NPS: target 52 (current 44)
- API uptime: 99.9 percent SLA
