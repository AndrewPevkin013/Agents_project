Investment Term Sheet
======================
Deal Reference: ITS-2025-EUR-089
Date: September 15, 2025
Jurisdiction: Netherlands (Dutch law)

TRANSACTION OVERVIEW
This term sheet outlines the proposed terms for a Series B investment round
in TechVenture LLC. The investment is structured as preferred equity with
participating liquidation preferences and standard investor protections.

FINANCIAL TERMS
Investment amount: 12,500,000 EUR
Pre-money valuation: 47,000,000 EUR
Post-money valuation: 59,500,000 EUR
Price per share: 18.40 EUR
New shares issued: 679,348 Series B Preferred Shares
Investor ownership post-close: 20.97 percent

FINANCIAL COVENANTS
- Minimum cash runway: 12 months at all times
- Maximum debt-to-equity ratio: 1.5x
- Quarterly financial reporting to board within 30 days of quarter close
- Annual audited financials required (Big 4 auditor)
- EBITDA positive by Q3 2026 (covenant breach triggers step-in rights)

LEGAL PROVISIONS
Liquidation preference: 1.5x non-participating preferred
Anti-dilution: Broad-based weighted average
Drag-along: 75 percent majority vote required
Tag-along: Pro-rata rights for all preferred shareholders
Board composition: 5 members — 2 founders, 2 investors, 1 independent
Information rights: Full access to books and records
Governing law: Kingdom of the Netherlands
Dispute resolution: Amsterdam arbitration (NAI rules)

CLOSING CONDITIONS
- Satisfactory legal due diligence completion
- Audited financials for FY2024 and Q1-Q2 2025
- Key employee retention agreements (3 year cliff vesting)
- No material adverse change between signing and closing
- Regulatory approval if required under Dutch FDI screening rules

TIMELINE
Term sheet signing: October 1, 2025
Due diligence period: 6 weeks
Target closing date: November 15, 2025
