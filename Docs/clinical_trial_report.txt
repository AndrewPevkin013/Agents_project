Clinical Trial Report: Phase II
=================================
Trial ID: CT-2025-NCT-04471
Sponsor: MediResearch Institute
Principal Investigator: Dr. Elena Marchetti
Date: August 2025

STUDY OVERVIEW
This Phase II randomized controlled trial evaluates the efficacy and safety
of compound MR-2247 in patients with moderate-to-severe rheumatoid arthritis.
Total enrollment: 284 patients across 12 clinical sites in Europe.

PRIMARY ENDPOINTS
- ACR50 response at week 24: 61.3 percent (treatment) vs 28.7 percent (placebo)
- p-value: < 0.001 (statistically significant)
- Number needed to treat (NNT): 3.1

SECONDARY ENDPOINTS
- DAS28-CRP reduction: -2.8 (treatment) vs -1.1 (placebo)
- HAQ-DI improvement: 0.62 points mean reduction
- Radiographic progression: inhibited in 78 percent of treatment group

SAFETY PROFILE
Adverse events (any grade): 42.3 percent treatment, 38.1 percent placebo
Serious adverse events: 4.2 percent treatment, 3.8 percent placebo
Most common: upper respiratory infection (12 percent), headache (8 percent)
No cases of opportunistic infection or malignancy observed.

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life: 14.2 hours
Bioavailability: 87 percent (oral administration)
Peak plasma concentration: 4.1 hours post-dose
Renal clearance: 34 percent unchanged

CONCLUSION
MR-2247 demonstrates clinically meaningful and statistically significant
improvement in RA disease activity with an acceptable safety profile.
Advancement to Phase III trials is recommended.
