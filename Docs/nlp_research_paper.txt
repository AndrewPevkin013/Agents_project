Natural Language Processing: State of the Art 2025
====================================================
Authors: Dr. Anna Kovac, Dr. James Liu
Published: March 2025, Journal of AI Research

ABSTRACT
This paper surveys the current state of natural language processing research,
covering transformer architectures, instruction tuning, RLHF, and emerging
multimodal language models. We analyze benchmark performance across 14 major
NLP tasks and identify key research gaps.

KEY FINDINGS
- Transformer scaling laws continue to hold up to 70B parameters
- Instruction-tuned models outperform base models by 31 percent on MMLU
- RLHF alignment significantly reduces harmful outputs (67 percent reduction)
- Multimodal models show 44 percent improvement on visual QA benchmarks

RESEARCH GAPS
1. Long-context reasoning beyond 128K tokens
2. Compositional generalization in low-resource languages
3. Robustness to adversarial prompt injection
4. Energy-efficient inference at scale

CONCLUSION
The field is rapidly advancing toward general-purpose language understanding.
Key challenges remain in alignment, efficiency, and compositional reasoning.
